# Sample Inputs for Testing

## Example 1: Diligence Snippet

**Case Type:** Diligence  
**Geography:** EU  
**Industry:** Technology  
**Stakes Level:** Client discussion

**Draft Text:**
```
The target company operates in a highly regulated environment. According to GDPR, all data processing activities must comply with strict requirements. The current data protection framework guarantees that customer data is secure. Recent enforcement actions have shown that non-compliance will result in significant fines.

The company's revenue has grown 45% year-over-year, reaching €250M in 2023. Market research indicates that the technology sector will continue to expand at this rate. The latest industry reports confirm our projections.

Key risks include potential regulatory changes in the EU AI Act, which could impact the company's AI-powered products. However, the management team has proven their ability to adapt to regulatory requirements.
```

## Example 2: Org Design Snippet

**Case Type:** Org Design  
**Geography:** US  
**Industry:** Financial Services  
**Stakes Level:** Board-level

**Draft Text:**
```
Our analysis reveals that the current organizational structure will deliver 30% cost savings within 12 months. The proposed restructuring guarantees improved efficiency and employee satisfaction.

According to research, companies that implement similar organizational changes see immediate benefits. The latest labor market data supports our recommendations. SEC disclosure requirements may need to be considered for public companies.

The transformation will require careful management of labor law compliance, particularly around workforce reductions. Our model proves that this approach is superior to alternatives.
```

## Example 3: Regulatory Snippet

**Case Type:** Strategy  
**Geography:** Global  
**Industry:** Healthcare  
**Stakes Level:** Client discussion

**Draft Text:**
```
The healthcare industry faces significant regulatory challenges. HIPAA compliance is mandatory for all US operations, and recent enforcement actions have increased penalties. The current regulatory environment will continue to evolve, requiring ongoing adaptation.

International operations must navigate varying data protection laws, including GDPR in Europe and emerging regulations in Asia. Our analysis shows that companies that invest in compliance infrastructure see better outcomes.

Market data indicates that healthcare spending will reach $4.5 trillion by 2025. This growth is driven by demographic trends and technological advances. The latest research confirms our strategic recommendations.
```
